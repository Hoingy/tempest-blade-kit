TempestBlade Kit is a Paper plugin re-imagining of the character Yasuo's kit from League of Legends in the context of Minecraft. The goal was to fit all the functionality
of the kit into Minecraft while adapting it to a 3D environment and Minecraft's theme.

Abilities:
**Passive**: Moving generates stacks. At 50 stacks, gain a small amount of absorption upon the next instance of damage.

**Steel Tempest**(Right-Click): Perform a quick, damaging thrust with an Iron Sword, building a stack upon successful hits. Stacks fall off after
  6 seconds. At 2 stacks, the next cast launches a tornado projectile forward, knocking up enemies hit. 
  
**Wind Wall**(F): Summon a forwards drifting wall that destroys all projectiles that touch it.

**Sweeping Blade**(Shift): Target the enemy you are looking at. Dash towards your target a set distance. This ability has a cooldown per target.
  Casting Steel Tempest during this ability will slash around you in a circle, applying Steel Tempest's damage, stacks, and knock up(if applicable).
  
**Last Breath(Q)**: Instantly teleport to the nearest knocked up or "Airborne" target. Suspend the target and any nearby airborne targets, dealing
  damage and slamming them to the ground.
